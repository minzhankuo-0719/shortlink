Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

ShortLink is a personal URL-shortening project. This page explains what data it collects, how that data is used, and how you can have it deleted. By signing in and using the service you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that provider. If you register with a username and password instead, we store your username and email.
  • Links you create. The destination URLs and optional titles of the short links you make.
  • Click analytics. When someone visits one of your short links, we record the visit's IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, and timestamp, so we can show you click statistics.

How we use it

We use this data only to operate the service: to authenticate you, to redirect short links to their destinations, and to show you the click analytics for your own links. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

We rely on a few third parties to run the service:

  • Google and Facebook — sign-in (OAuth). Their own privacy policies govern the data they hold.
  • Google Cloud — application hosting and database.
  • Upstash — caching (Redis).

Data retention

Your links and their click history are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting a link removes it and its click history.

How to delete your data

  • Delete a single link (and its clicks): open your dashboard (My Links) and click the trash icon on that link.
  • Delete your entire account and all associated data: email us at the address below from the address on your account and we will permanently delete your account, your links, and their click history.
